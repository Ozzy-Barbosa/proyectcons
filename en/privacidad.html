Information about the contact form in this preview.

What information is used

The form uses your name, phone number and project details you choose to provide to prepare an enquiry for PROJECTCONS. It does not request identity documents, bank details or file uploads.

When information is shared

The summary is prepared in your browser. It is not stored in a website database or sent automatically. Clicking “Open WhatsApp” transfers the text to that service; you confirm sending it in the chat. WhatsApp has its own privacy policy.

Contact and follow-up

PROJECTCONS will receive only what you send through WhatsApp to respond to your enquiry and discuss your project. For questions or to request that follow-up stop, contact +52 612 136 3583.

External services

Hosting is provided by GitHub Pages. Fonts load from Google Fonts. Photography credit links lead to Pexels. No analytics tools or advertising pixels have been added. These providers may process technical data when their resources load or their links are visited.
