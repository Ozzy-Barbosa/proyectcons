05BEFORE YOU BUILD

The questions
that matter.

I want to build a house in La Paz. Where do I start?+

Tell us where your lot is, what kind of home you have in mind and where you are in the process. If you already have plans or references, share them on WhatsApp to discuss the next steps.

What information do I need to request an estimate?+

Location, project type, approximate floor area if known, indicative budget and preferred timeline. An estimate requires a scope review; the form does not generate an automatic price.

How much does it cost to build in La Paz, BCS?+

There is no one-size-fits-all price. The lot, floor area, design, finishes and site conditions all matter. Understanding these details helps shape a proposal for your project.

Can I enquire about a project outside La Paz?+

Yes. Include the location in Baja California Sur when you contact us. Availability and scope are reviewed for each project.

Are the example photos PROJECTCONS projects?+

No. Our own photography is labelled as the PROJECTCONS portfolio. Images marked as visual references are third-party examples used temporarily while each project’s information is completed.
